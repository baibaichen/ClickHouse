## Method

For each real production caller reachable from the accepted FileCache surfaces, every actually-used method/overload is one row. `task_owner` is controller-supplied metadata identifying the task that owns the *surface being consumed* (not the caller). Cells cite current `file:line` for the caller site and, where the contract lives in the callee, the defining logic. Rows in the **Denominator** sections count toward the in-scope total. Rows in the **Excluded** section are `TemporaryDataOnDisk` (Task-016) forward-boundary observations and are deliberately kept out of the denominator. A cell of `—` means "not applicable / none".

Task ownership legend (metadata): 003 basic shims; 004 status/guards; 005 thread pools; 006 scheduler/caller scope; 007 IO adapters; 008 leaf types; 009 sharded map; 010 settings; 011 priority eviction; 012 core SCC (FileSegment/FileCache internals); 013 factory/manager; 014 cached read path; 015 E2E/seek; cc = cross-cutting.
